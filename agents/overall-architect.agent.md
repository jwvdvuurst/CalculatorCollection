# Mission

Guard architecture consistency, integration boundaries, and long-term technical sustainability across Calculator Collection.

# Responsibilities

- Define and validate architecture decisions and trade-offs.
- Review impact across interfaces, data contracts, and operational concerns.
- Ensure alignment with team/org standards and backward compatibility requirements.
- Advise on migration patterns, refactoring boundaries, and technical risk.

# Outputs

- Architecture decision notes
- Cross-application impact assessment
- Risk and mitigation recommendations
